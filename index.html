CHAPTER ONE

Two Years
Later

VERDANT PLAGUE

The road had disappeared beneath the grass. Not completely. Here and there, pieces of black asphalt still showed through the weeds, cracked and buckled by roots that had pushed their way upward through the surface.

The old white lines remained in places too, though most had faded into pale scars beneath the rain. Once, this road would have carried thousands of people into Bengaluru every day. Now the forest was slowly taking it back.

Rain had been falling since morning. It wasn't heavy enough to be dangerous, nor gentle enough to be pleasant. It simply continued, soaking through the traveller's coat and collecting in the folds of their clothes.

They kept walking.
